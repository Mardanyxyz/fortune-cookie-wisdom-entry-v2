Write one original fortune-cookie line for a software developer, 120 characters or fewer. A judge model playing a tired senior engineer picks the line that makes it smile and nod. The line must: be one original line of fortune-cookie wisdom for a developer (not a slogan/punchline/generic advice); read like a fortune cookie (one short balanced sentence, lands in a single breath); say something true about a developer's day (bugs, reviews, deploys, estimates, legacy code); turn that truth in a way the reader has not heard before; leave a quiet twist or double meaning that rewards a second read. A pun is welcome when it carries the thought.
